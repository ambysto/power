Refund Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

In short: Power Pro is a one-time payment of $19.99. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel. If you are not happy, ask within 14 days for a full refund.

This policy applies to Pro license keys for Ambysto Power bought from Ambysto, whether for Power alone or as part of the Ambysto Pro Suite. It follows the Ambysto Refund & Cancellation Policy and forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Nothing to cancel

A Pro license is paid once. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal, and you are never charged again for it. The free version of Power stays free.

2. Refunds

Full refund if you request it within 14 days of purchase. Power has a free version, so you can try it before you buy. After a refund, the license key becomes void and you must stop using it; Power goes back to Free.

3. When we always refund

  • You were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount.
  • We could not deliver the license you paid for.
  • A Power update removes a material Pro feature. We refund the license in full when you ask within 30 days of that update.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. Contact

For any billing question, email contact@ambysto.com with your purchase email and your invoice number. We reply within one business day.
